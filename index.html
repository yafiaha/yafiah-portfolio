03

Accessible forms & document infrastructure

Ongoing
Challenge

The county relies on third-party platforms to deliver public forms and documents, but some platform-generated HTML, ARIA markup, form controls, and document workflows introduced accessibility barriers that couldn't be corrected through standard CMS or form-builder interfaces.

What I did

I developed a testing and escalation workflow for identifying accessibility defects — distinguishing content-level issues from vendor-generated platform issues, documenting failures, testing proposed fixes, and coordinating remediation with software vendors.

My role

I conduct technical accessibility testing, reproduce issues across multiple testing tools, document affected components, evaluate available workarounds, and work directly with vendors when remediation requires changes to platform-generated code.

Approach
SimpliGov DocAccess WAVE Accessibility Insights Adobe Acrobat Pro HTML/ARIA analysis WCAG 2.1 AA
Impact

Created a repeatable method for separating editor-remediable accessibility issues from platform defects, letting the county escalate technical problems with substantially better evidence.

Testing and escalation workflow: content issues remediated internally, platform issues escalated to the vendor, both validated and monitored ongoing
